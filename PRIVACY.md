# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

This policy covers the **JSONL to JSON Converter** Chrome extension.

## Summary

The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. Everything happens locally in your browser.

## Data handling

- **Files and pasted text.** Content you open or paste is processed in memory inside the extension's own page and a Web Worker bundled with the extension. It is never uploaded or sent anywhere, and it is discarded when you close the side panel or tab.
- **No storage.** The extension saves nothing to disk, browser storage, or sync storage. Files are only written when you click Save, through your browser's normal download.
- **No network requests.** The extension makes no network connections and loads no remote code.
- **No analytics or tracking.** There are no analytics, telemetry, cookies, or advertising identifiers.
- **No sale or sharing.** No user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than the conversion you asked for.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| `sidePanel` | Shows the converter in Chrome's side panel when you click the toolbar icon. It does not read the content of web pages. |
| `clipboardWrite` | Lets the **Copy** button put the converted output on your clipboard. This happens only when you click the button. |

The extension requests no host permissions and cannot access the websites you visit.
